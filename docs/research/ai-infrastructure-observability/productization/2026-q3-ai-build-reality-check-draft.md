# AI Build Reality Check — 2026 Q3 draft

> **NVIDIA shows that AI chip demand is strong. This brief checks whether major AI infrastructure
> projects are actually contracted, powered, under construction, or operating—and where execution
> is at risk.**

**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-08-30

**Coverage:** fixed 15-project known-case cohort; not a complete global inventory

## Current answer

**Demand is strong. Physical execution is real but uneven.**

- NVIDIA Data Center revenue was USD 89.0B in FY2027 Q2, up 117% year over year.
- The existing four-hyperscaler CapEx baseline is positive, with positive TSMC historical and
  forward confirmation.
- Of 15 tracked projects, 7 are operating, 7 are under construction or development, and 1 is
  contracted without supported construction evidence.
- The project evidence does not establish that global physical buildout is accelerating or
  decelerating; this is the first fixed-cohort baseline.

## What you can decide in five minutes

The demand evidence supports continued AI infrastructure spending. The project evidence shows that
real facilities are progressing, while identifying execution facts that the demand headline alone
cannot reveal:

| Project | What the evidence adds | Plain-language status |
|---|---|---|
| Michigan / The Barn | Construction has started, but the critical-power contracts are conditionally approved and under active appeal | Building, with an unresolved power-contract risk |
| Osaka Sakai | The original 2025 operation target was revised to 2026; reported 140/150 MW figures are receiving power, not AI IT load | Building, but later than originally announced |
| AWS Project Rainier | Nearly 500,000 Trainium2 chips were reported fully operational, providing a non-NVIDIA physical-build observation | Operating with project-specific compute evidence |

### Verify the evidence

- **Michigan:** [MPSC conditional approval](https://www.michigan.gov/mpsc/commission/news-releases/2025/12/18/mpsc-approves-dte-electric-energy-contracts-for-data-center),
  [OpenAI groundbreaking](https://openai.com/index/stargate-michigan-data-center/), and
  [Michigan Attorney General appeal](https://www.michigan.gov/ag/news/press-releases/2026/04/17/ag-nessel-files-appeal-of-dtes-saline-data-center-contracts).
  These sources support construction and risk; they do not establish that 1 GW campus power or
  1,383 MW contracted electric demand is AI IT load.
- **Sakai:** [SoftBank construction description](https://www.softbank.jp/sbnews/entry/20260609_01)
  and the existing evidence dossier preserve the 2025-to-2026 schedule revision. The latest
  receiving-power description is not converted into AI compute capacity.
- **Rainier:** [Amazon's operational announcement](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/aws/aws-project-rainier-ai-trainium-chips-compute-cluster)
  supports a project-specific Trainium2 observation. A later collaboration-wide quantity is not
  added to this project.

## What this quarter can and cannot tell you

**It can tell you:**

- headline AI demand is supported by spending, semiconductor, and project-level evidence;
- physical deployment is not merely an announcement story; and
- specific projects can carry schedule, power-contract, or scope risks even while demand is strong.

**It cannot yet tell you:**

- whether global physical buildout is accelerating or decelerating;
- the total global AI compute or power capacity;
- whether the public known-case cohort represents the wider market; or
- whether project evidence consistently improves a decision beyond NVIDIA, CSP, and TSMC evidence.

## What to watch next quarter

The same 15 projects will be checked for only six evidence-backed changes: a binding contract,
critical power secured, construction start, equipment installation, commissioned operation, or a
delay/scope reduction/cancellation. No new projects are needed for this test.

The next brief must answer one question:

> **Did project-level evidence change or qualify the conclusion available from NVIDIA, CSP, and TSMC
> evidence alone?**

If two quarterly updates only repeat the same headline demand conclusion, stop this project layer.

---

**Research limitations:** public known-project cohort; North America is overrepresented; Africa and
South America have no retained row; immutable source versions, independent corroboration, local-
language coverage, and permitted automated reuse remain incomplete. This brief is not investment
advice and makes no complete-global-capacity claim.
