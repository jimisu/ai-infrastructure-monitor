# WP8M value, closest-tracker, and effort evaluation

## Closest-tracker comparison

| Case | Closest R1 landscape baseline | What an inventory/headline view tends to retain | Increment demonstrated by this micro-pilot |
|---|---|---|---|
| xAI Colossus | U.S. Data Center Atlas / IM3 as location inventory; ordinary operator/supplier headlines | Facility/location or headline GPU quantities | Prevents 100k, 180k, 200k and 1m from being added; preserves configuration, conflict, lifecycle and source origin |
| Munich Industrial AI Cloud | Sovereign AI Tracker as initiative discovery; ordinary launch headlines | Initiative status/funding or headline 10k | Separates announcement, installation and launch; rejects nearly/up-to 10k as proof of the strict threshold while retaining commissioned AI-explicit status |

External tracker rows were not copied or promoted. This is a structural capability comparison based on the R1 compatibility review, not a claim that every current tracker row was exhaustively audited.

## Decision improvement

The timeline prevents a decision-maker from treating headline maxima as commissioned large-project capacity. In these two cases that changes the usable result from an apparent sum of multiple xAI expansion numbers plus an assumed Munich 10k into one supported 100k lower-bound and one threshold-unresolved commissioned case.

## Actual-effort record and limitations

- WP4M through WP9M was conducted in one authorized session with staged Git checkpoints.
- Agent/tool wall-clock time includes access waits, repository writes, test runs, and one executor usage-limit interruption; it is not equivalent to human analyst hours.
- Exact discovery, extraction, classification and correction minutes were not instrumented and are not reconstructed.
- Therefore no mature weekly or quarterly maintenance cost is claimed. The missing time instrumentation is a closeout limitation and would need explicit instrumentation in full R2.

## Verdict

`MICRO_PILOT_VALUE_DEMONSTRATED`

Basis: the pilot demonstrated at least three required incremental capabilities — duplicate prevention, planned-versus-confirmed separation, and precision-safe timeline treatment — plus a material decision improvement. The verdict does not establish global coverage, legal permission for automated acquisition, mature maintenance cost, production readiness, or R2 completion.
