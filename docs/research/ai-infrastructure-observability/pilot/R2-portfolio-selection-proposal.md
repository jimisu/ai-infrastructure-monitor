## 1. Boundary and screen method

This artifact completes WP4 selection only. It proposes exactly five depth cases and a 20-project
candidate seed. It is not a verified global timeline, a statistical sample, a source registry, a set
of dossiers, a lifecycle or capacity classification, or an aggregate. Candidate quantities and
descriptions below are discovery cues requiring exact-source verification; they are not accepted
facts.

The shallow screen considered: an identifiable official operator, government, scientific,
procurement, utility or supplier channel; likely independent-origin corroboration; region and
language; expected campus/project/phase/tranche and capacity-basis problems; public access without an
obvious sign-in or payment wall; and whether the case could exercise the approved evidence rules.
The screen reused the official locators already observed in WP4M and added official-channel leads for
breadth. It did not preserve pages, translate documents, extract claims, inspect complete terms or
`robots.txt`, or determine final eligibility.

Public reachability is not permission for automated collection or redistribution. Every selected
source still needs a WP5 permitted-method record covering terms, `robots.txt` where relevant,
authentication/payment boundaries, automated-access restrictions, copyright/reuse, stable version
behavior and jurisdiction-specific restrictions. Any source marked `access unknown` must be manually
rechecked or replaced before acquisition. No snapshot or translation is committed.

## 5. Inclusion, reserve and exclusion rationale

- The five selected cases satisfy all five named depth channels with no case counted twice to evade a
  missing category. They deliberately mix evidence-rich positives with threshold, independence and
  program/phase stress rather than selecting only easily quantified English cases.
- Abilene, Fairwater, Tomakomai, Isambard-AI and JUPITER are the strongest immediate reserves because
  official channels and a bounded physical system or phase appear discoverable. They remain reserves
  to keep the depth set exactly five and avoid North American/European overconcentration.
- The remaining alternates broaden region, language and operator type but have material unresolved AI
  attribution, power-basis, phase, access or source-independence questions. Those are screening risks,
  not final exclusions from a future research dataset.
- Saudi HUMAIN/AMD is excluded now because the visible lead is a multi-year infrastructure program
  without a sufficiently bounded physical project in this screen. Kenya is excluded now as an MOU or
  initiative negative case. Neither may enter confirmed expected capacity without later eligible
  evidence.
- No external tracker row was copied. Discovery aids remain discovery-only under the R1 compatibility
  verdict and cannot replace original official evidence.

## 6. Feasibility verdict and next gate

`PORTFOLIO_SELECTION_FEASIBLE_PENDING_HUMAN_APPROVAL`

The proposal is regionally and linguistically varied enough to ask the five-case evidence questions,
and the 20-project seed supplies breadth for a later qualifying screen. It does not establish that 15
or more projects will pass the confirmed-build, AI-attribution, large-threshold or source-independence
rules. The expected highest risks are program/campus/phase identity, utility or receiving-power
substitution, mutable official pages, shared-origin partner releases, and Japanese/Korean/Arabic/
Portuguese source access.
