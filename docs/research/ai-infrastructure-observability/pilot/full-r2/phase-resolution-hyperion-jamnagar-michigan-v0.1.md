## Resolution rule

A case can return to the qualifying population only if the evidence identifies one stable physical
project, independently executable phase, or secured critical-power/commissioning tranche that:

1. does not overlap the campus, another project, or a later expansion;
2. has its own `CONTRACTED`, `PROCURED`, critical-power-secured, or `UNDER_CONSTRUCTION` evidence;
3. has project-specific AI attribution; and
4. preserves facility, utility, and campus power outside AI IT-load capacity.

## Case 22 — Michigan / The Barn secured-power tranche

### Resolved minimum identity

`GREEN_CHILE_SALINE_FACILITY_U_21990_SECURED_POWER_TRANCHE`

The minimum unit is the Saline Township data-center facility to be leased and operated by Green
Chile Ventures LLC under the DTE Primary Supply Agreement and Energy Storage Agreement in MPSC case
U-21990. This is a contract-defined critical-power tranche linked to one legal customer/facility,
not the entire Stargate program or an inferred building phase.

It excludes individual battery projects, wider DTE system resources, and any conversion of the
contracted utility load into AI IT load.
