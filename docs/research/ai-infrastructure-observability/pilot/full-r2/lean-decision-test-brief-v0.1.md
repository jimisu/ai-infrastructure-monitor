## What the cheaper baseline already answers

The implemented Demand Layer is positive across all four eligible hyperscalers and has positive TSMC
historical/forward confirmation. NVIDIA FY2027 Q2 makes the same broad conclusion even more directly:
Data Center revenue was USD 89.0B, up 117% year over year; supply/capacity commitments increased from
USD 119B to USD 279B; and management described demand as accelerating.

NVIDIA now also says land, power, and shell are a critical buildout phase. Its Form 10-Q records
long-term cloud, lease, financing, land/power/shell, and customer-support commitments, while warning
that customers can be delayed by infrastructure availability, capital, permitting, regulation,
community opposition, construction execution, or weaker end demand.

Therefore these statements are **not** sufficient Track B differentiation:

- AI infrastructure demand is strong;
- power matters;
- data centers take time to build; or
- customers face execution risk.

NVIDIA already discloses all four.

## Why the scope should shrink

The previous tabletop estimated 19.1–34.2 human hours for a full 22-case quarterly review. That cost
is not justified merely to repeat NVIDIA's conclusion. The next two updates should:

1. keep the 15-case cohort fixed;
2. check only the six authorized transition categories;
3. deep-review only changed, conflicting, unavailable, or stale rows;
4. publish no capacity total;
5. record actual human time; and
6. produce one sentence explaining whether Track B changed the cheaper baseline.

No new hourly estimate is claimed before this lean procedure is measured.

## Two-quarter stop test

After each update, answer these questions:

1. Did Track B identify a project-level delay, scope reduction, cancellation, regulatory conflict,
   or commissioning transition not visible in NVIDIA/CSP/TSMC evidence?
2. Did it change the classification from generic demand strength to `SUPPLY_CONSTRAINED`,
   `EXECUTION_AT_RISK`, or another evidence-backed qualification?
3. Did it capture a material non-NVIDIA build transition?
4. Did the result change what the user would investigate, hold constant, or refuse to infer?

Continue beyond two quarters only if at least two questions are answered `YES` with cited evidence
and the measured maintenance burden remains acceptable. If all observed changes merely confirm the
same positive NVIDIA/CSP/TSMC direction, stop Track B and retain only the cheaper baseline.
